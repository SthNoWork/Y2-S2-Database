- Fruit Relation
In TP01, you organized information about fruits in a grocery shop. The shop has apples,
bananas, and oranges. There are ten apples, and three of them are rotten while the rest are
fresh. There are five bananas, and all of them are fresh. There are eight oranges, of which
two are rotten and the rest are fresh.
Your task is to convert this grouped information into a relation that stores the data clearly
with no unnecessary repetition.
