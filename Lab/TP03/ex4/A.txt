Part A:
Explain why a hierarchical model is NOT suitable for this scenario.

answer:

Hospital data is a bit of a spiderweb, which makes it the perfect candidate for this analysis.
A hierarchical model is like a rigid "Tree" where every child can only have one parent.

In a hospital:
A patient is often treated by many doctors (surgeons, nurses...)

A doctor treats many patients.

If you used a hierarchical model, you would have to choose:
Is the doctor the parent or the patient?
If the doctor is the parent, you'd have to duplicate a patient's entire file under every doctor they see.
It’s a mess of redundant data that would eventually lead to errors.
