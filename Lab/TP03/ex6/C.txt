Part C:
Why did you separate Orders and Order details into two relations instead of putting everything in one table?
Explain in 2-3 sentences.

answer:
If you put everything in one table, you would have to repeat the OrderDate, CustomerName, and TotalAmount for every single item in the order.
If a customer orders 10 different sushi rolls, you'd have 10 rows of redundant information.

By separating them, you store the "receipt header" info once in Orders and list the "line items" in OrderDetails,
which saves storage space and prevents mistakes when updating data.
