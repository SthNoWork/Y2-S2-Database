Part C: The Limitation
A pure hierarchical model is strictly limited by the "One Parent Rule,"
meaning a child record (the Professor) can only be linked to a single parent record (the Department).

If a professor works in multiple departments, the hierarchical model fails because it cannot support multiple parents for one child.
To fix this, you would have to duplicate the professor's data in every department they work for,
which leads to data redundancy and potential errors if their information changes.
