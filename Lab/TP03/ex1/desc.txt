Exercise 1 - Choosing the Data Model

For each situation below, choose the model that fits best:
hierarchical model, network model, or relational model.

• A company stores departments and employees where each employee belongs to only one department.
• A social platform stores users and friendships, where one user can be connected to many other users.
• A school stores students in tables with rows and columns.
• A file system on your computer organizes folders and files in a parent-child structure.
• A university tracks which students take which courses, where students can take many courses and each course has many students.

For each situation, write one short sentence to explain your choice.

Hint: Consider the relationship pattern (one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many).
