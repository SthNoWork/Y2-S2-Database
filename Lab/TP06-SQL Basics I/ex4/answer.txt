Exercise 4 – Weak Entity Discussion

1. Why can orderdetails be considered a weak entity?

   Because it cannot exist on its own. An order detail line only makes sense
   if there is an order and a product behind it. It has no independent
   primary key — its identity is a combination of orderNumber and productCode,
   both of which are foreign keys to other tables. Remove the parent order
   or product and the detail has no meaning.

2. What makes productlines a strong entity?

   Because it exists completely on its own. A product line like "Classic Cars"
   or "Motorcycles" is a standalone category that does not depend on any other
   table to exist. It has its own primary key (productLine) and nothing in its
   definition references another table.

3. The employees table has a column reportsTo that references another row in
   the same table. What kind of relationship is this called?
   
   This is called a self-referencing relationship (also called a recursive
   relationship). It means one employee can be the manager of another employee,
   and both the manager and the subordinate live in the same table. The
   reportsTo column stores the employeeNumber of the manager.
