Exercise 5 - ER Diagram with Multiple Entities

A company manages employees and projects. Employees work on projects,
and the company needs to track which employees are assigned to which projects.
Each employee can work on multiple projects simultaneously,
and each project requires multiple employees.

The company needs to store:
• employee information (ID, name, position, salary, department)
• project information (ID, name, budget, start date).

Your task is to:
• Identify all entities and their attributes
• Identify the relationship between entities
• Determine the cardinality
• Draw a complete ER diagram
