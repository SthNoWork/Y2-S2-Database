Exercise 3 - Identifying Relationships and Cardinality

For each situation below, identify the relationship between entities and determine the cardinality
    (one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many).
• A department employs many teachers. Each teacher works in one department.
• A student can enroll in multiple courses. Each course can have multiple students.
• A country has one president. Each president leads one country.
• A customer can place multiple orders. Each order is placed by one customer.

Hint: Read the relationship in both directions to determine cardinality.
