Exercise 4 - Simple ER Diagram

A gym wants to manage its members and personal trainers.
Each member joins the gym and is assigned to one personal trainer for fitness guidance.
Each trainer is responsible for multiple members.

The gym needs to track information about:
• members(ID, name, join, date, phone)
• trainers (ID, name, specialty, hourly rate).

Your task is to:
• Identify the entities and their attributes
• Identify the relationship between entities
• Determine the cardinality
• Draw an ER diagram

Hint: Use rectangles for entities, diamonds for relationships, lines for connections.
