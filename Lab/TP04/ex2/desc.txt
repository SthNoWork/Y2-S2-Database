Exercise 2 - Determining Attributes

A university needs a course registration system. The system must:
• Track student information so the university can contact them and verify their enrollment
• Store course information so students know what courses are available and how many credits they earn
• Record instructor details so the university knows who teaches each course

Your task is to list appropriate attributes for Student, Course, and Instructor entities.

Hint: Each entity needs a unique identifier and properties that describe it
