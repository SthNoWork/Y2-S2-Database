Exercise 6 – GROUP BY with JOIN

1. Show each customerName and the total number of orders they have placed. Include customers with zero orders (use LEFT JOIN). Sort by order count descending.
2. Show each productName and the total quantity ordered across all orders. Sort by total quantity descending.
3. Show each officeCode (city), the number of employees in that office, and the number of customers those employees manage in total.
4. For each order, calculate the total order value (SUM(quantityOrdered * priceEach)). Show orderNumber and orderTotal, sorted by orderTotal descending.
