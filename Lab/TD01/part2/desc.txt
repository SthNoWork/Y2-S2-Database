– Network Model
A company database needs to store information about employees (identified by ssn, with
salary and phone as attributes), departments (identified by dnu, with dname and budget
as attributes), and children of employees (with name and age as attributes). Employees
work in departments; each department is managed by an employee; a child must be
identified uniquely by name when the parent (who is an employee; assume that only one
parent works for the company) is known. We are not interested in information about a
child once the parent leaves the company.

Design using network model for the database above.
