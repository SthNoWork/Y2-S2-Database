Exercise 7 - Answers

1. Repeated information:
   - Customer info (CustomerName, CustomerPhone) repeats once per sale.
     Dara + 012-111-111 appears in rows 1001 and 1002.
     Sophea + 012-222-222 appears in rows 1003 and 1004.
   - Product info (ProductName, Price) repeats across rows.
     Notebook, 2.50 appears in rows 1001 and 1003.
     Pen, 0.75 appears in rows 1002 and 1005.

2. Update problem:
   If Dara changes her phone number from 012-111-111 to 012-999-999, rows 1001
   and 1002 must both be updated. If only row 1001 is updated, the same customer
   appears to have two different phone numbers — an inconsistent, corrupted state.
   This is an UPDATE ANOMALY caused by storing customer data inside the sale rows.

3. Better decomposition:

   Customer(CustomerID, CustomerName, CustomerPhone)
   Product(ProductID, ProductName, Price)
   Sale(SaleID, CustomerID, ProductID, Quantity)

   --- Customer table ---
   CustomerID | CustomerName | CustomerPhone
   -----------|--------------|---------------
   C01        | Dara         | 012-111-111
   C02        | Sophea       | 012-222-222
   C03        | Vanna        | 012-333-333

   --- Product table ---
   ProductID | ProductName | Price
   ----------|-------------|------
   P01       | Notebook    | 2.50
   P02       | Pen         | 0.75
   P03       | Eraser      | 0.50

   --- Sale table ---
   SaleID | CustomerID | ProductID | Quantity
   -------|------------|-----------|----------
   1001   | C01        | P01       | 3
   1002   | C01        | P02       | 10
   1003   | C02        | P01       | 1
   1004   | C02        | P03       | 5
   1005   | C03        | P02       | 2

   Benefits:
   - Updating Dara's phone now requires changing exactly 1 row in Customer.
   - Changing a product price requires changing exactly 1 row in Product.
   - No customer or product information is lost if a sale row is deleted.
