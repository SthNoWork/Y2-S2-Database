Exercise 3 - Insertion and Deletion Problems

Use the same relation:
  StudentCourse(StudentID, StudentName, City, CourseID, CourseName, Score)

Situation A - Insertion:
  A new student S04 (Chenda, Kampot) registers but has not enrolled in any course yet.
  S04 | Chenda | Kampot | ??? | ??? | ???

Situation B - Deletion:
  Vanna (S03) is the only student taking English (C3).
  Deleting that row removes: (S03, Vanna, Phnom Penh, C3, English, 65)

Questions:
  1. What problem happens when adding a student with no course enrollment?
  2. What problem happens when deleting the only row for one course?
