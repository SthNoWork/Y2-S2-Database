Exercise 3 - Answers

1. Insertion Anomaly:
   Student S04 (Chenda, Kampot) cannot be inserted because CourseID and CourseName
   are part of the relation and cannot be left NULL if they form part of the primary key.
   We are forced to either leave required fields blank (invalid) or invent a fake course
   just to store the student's information.
   This is called an INSERTION ANOMALY: we cannot record one fact without also
   providing an unrelated fact.

2. Deletion Anomaly:
   Deleting the row (S03, Vanna, Phnom Penh, C3, English, 65) removes the only
   record of course C3 (English) from the entire database.
   We lose Vanna's student record AND the English course information at the same time,
   even though we only intended to remove an enrollment.
   This is called a DELETION ANOMALY: removing one fact accidentally destroys a
   different, unrelated fact.
