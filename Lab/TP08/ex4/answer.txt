Exercise 4 - Answer

Decomposed Relations:

  Student(StudentID, StudentName, City)
  Course(CourseID, CourseName)
  Enrollment(StudentID, CourseID, Score)

--- Student table ---
StudentID | StudentName | City
----------|-------------|------------
S01       | Dara        | Phnom Penh
S02       | Sophea      | Siem Reap
S03       | Vanna       | Phnom Penh

--- Course table ---
CourseID | CourseName
---------|----------
C1       | Math
C2       | Physics
C3       | English

--- Enrollment table ---
StudentID | CourseID | Score
----------|----------|------
S01       | C1       | 85
S01       | C2       | 78
S01       | C3       | 90
S02       | C1       | 70
S02       | C2       | 88
S03       | C3       | 65

Verification:
  JOIN Student + Enrollment ON StudentID,
  then JOIN result + Course ON CourseID
  reproduces the full original StudentCourse relation with no data loss.

Benefits of this decomposition:
  - Student name/city is stored exactly once per student (no redundant rows).
  - Course name is stored exactly once per course.
  - Updating Dara's city now requires changing exactly 1 row in Student.
  - Adding a new student with no courses is now possible (insert into Student only).
  - Deleting an enrollment row does not remove the student or course record.
