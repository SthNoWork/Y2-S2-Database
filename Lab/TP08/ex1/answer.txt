Exercise 1 - Answers

1. Repeated information:
   - Student information (StudentID, StudentName, City) repeats once per course the
     student is enrolled in.
   - Course information (CourseID, CourseName) repeats once per student enrolled in
     that course.

2. Example of repeated student information:
   S01, Dara, Phnom Penh appears three times (once for Math, Physics, and English).

3. Example of repeated course information:
   C1, Math appears twice (once for S01 Dara and once for S02 Sophea).
