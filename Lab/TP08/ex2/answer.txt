Update Anomaly:
  Because Dara's city is stored in multiple rows (one per course enrolled), updating
  the city requires changing every row that belongs to S01.

  If only one row is updated (e.g., C1 row is changed to Battambang but C2 and C3
  rows are not updated), the table becomes inconsistent — the same student appears
  to live in two different cities at the same time.

  This is called an UPDATE ANOMALY: a single real-world change must be applied to
  many rows, and forgetting even one row corrupts the data.
